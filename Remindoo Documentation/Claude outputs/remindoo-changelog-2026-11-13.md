# Payments, Calendar and WhatsApp – 13 November 2026

This release adds a Calendar that syncs with Google Calendar, WhatsApp messaging, new ways to get paid through GoCardless and PayPal, invoice refunds, a new Activity view on your records and live chat with the Remindoo team.

## New

- **Calendar** – A new Calendar in Remindoo. Connect your Google account to keep your events in sync with Google Calendar. You can still create events without connecting Google, and Remindoo asks you to confirm first.
- **Whole App** – A new Activity tab on Tasks, Clients, Leads and Proposals shows the record's history on a single timeline, grouped by day, with a filter for each type of event. The existing timeline tab works as before.
- **Whole App** – Chat with the Remindoo team from inside the app. Open Chat with us from What's new, and you'll see an unread badge and a notification when we reply.
- **Clients and Tasks** – WhatsApp: message your clients on WhatsApp from Remindoo, with the conversation kept on the client's record.
- **Invoices** – GoCardless: collect invoice payments by Direct Debit.
- **Invoices** – PayPal: clients can pay their invoices with PayPal.
- **Invoices** – Refund a paid invoice in full or in part. Payments made through Stripe or GoCardless are refunded through that provider, and other payments are recorded in Remindoo. Each refund creates a credit note, the invoice shows Refunded or Partially refunded, and the refund syncs to your accounting software.
- **Settings** – Choose your firm's currency and date format in Global Settings. Your currency shows wherever amounts appear and is the default for new invoices, and every date in the app follows your chosen format. Changing the currency only changes how amounts are labelled; existing amounts aren't converted.
- **Integrations and AI** – Connect Remindoo to AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT, and ask questions about your practice in plain English. Access is read-only and follows each user's permissions.

## Improved

- **Clients** – The direct debit status in the invoice window now stays in sync with GoCardless.
- **Leads** – Notes now appear in the lead's Activity tab.
- **Invoices** – Creating an invoice automatically when a proposal is signed is now optional, so you can switch it off.
- **Whole App** – Behind the scenes, the Remindoo team's admin portal has a new sign-in with roles and two-step verification. Your own sign-in doesn't change.

## Fixed

- **Leads** – The filter window now displays correctly, and the Assignee filter shows names instead of IDs.
