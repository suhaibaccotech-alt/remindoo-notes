# Automations — Workflows

**Purpose:** Step-by-step instructions for the common tasks users perform in the **Automations** module.

## Add a rule in the table
1. **Settings → Automations**, then pick the tab (Leads, Proposals, Tasks, Clients or Services). On Tasks, also pick **Status** or **Deadlines**.
2. Click **Add Rule**. A new row appears, paused.
3. Fill the row in from left to right: the status (or, for deadlines, which task statuses and which deadline), how long to wait, the action (e.g. **Send Email**), the email template, and who it goes to.
   - For a deadline rule you choose the statuses (*All open statuses*, Not started, In-Progress, Awaiting Feedback), **Internal** or **External deadline**, a number of days, and **Before** or **After**.
4. Switch **Active** on when you're ready for it to run.
5. Click **Save Automations**. Nothing is kept until you do.

## Build a rule visually
1. Click **Build visually** and choose what the rule is for: Leads, Proposals, Tasks, Task deadlines, Clients or Services.
2. Add the **trigger** first, then the timing, then the action. Drag from a step's right-hand dot to the next step to connect them. Click any step to set it up.
3. To change a connection, drag either end of the arrow onto another step, or click its **×** to remove it.
4. Choose whether the rule is **Active on save**, then **Add to grid** to put it in the table, and **Save automations**.

## Pause or resume a rule
Use the **Active** switch on its row, then **Save Automations**.
