# Roles & Permissions

**Purpose:** How to use the **Roles & Permissions** feature, part of the **Settings** module.

**Settings → Employee Roles & Permissions** controls what each role can see and do. Each employee's role is shown in **Employee List**.

## How the page works
- Each role is a column (e.g. **Admin**, **Employee**, or one your firm has added with **Create Role**), showing how many permissions it has out of the total and how many users have it (e.g. *Admin 79/80 permissions, 2 users*).
- Each module is a row, showing how many of its permissions the role has (e.g. *Clients 7/7*). Click a row, or **Expand All**, to see and tick the individual permissions.
- Modules: Dashboard, Clients, Tasks, Leads, Knowledge Base, Proposals, Document Portal, Invoices, Calendar, Email, WhatsApp and Settings.
- **Dashboard** is set per tab (Tasks, Timesheet, Capacity Planning, Leads, Proposals, Employee & Services, Client Summary). For each tab you choose the **Data Visibility** (e.g. *All*) and whether the role can **Export CSV**.

## What a missing permission looks like
If a role has no permissions for a module, people with that role don't see the module in the left menu, and opening its page shows a message such as "Access denied – You don't have permission to view clients."

## If someone can't see a module
Check their role here first. A module missing from their menu almost always means the role has none of that module's permissions ticked.
