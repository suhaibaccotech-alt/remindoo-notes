# Dashboard — Overview

**Purpose:** What the **Dashboard** shows and how to read it.

The Dashboard is the first page after login ("Welcome <name>"). It has seven tabs: **Tasks**, **Timesheet**, **Capacity Planning**, **Leads**, **Proposals**, **Employee & Services** and **Client Summary**.

## Tasks tab
- A service filter (all services or one) and History / Current / Upcoming.
- Stage cards: **Info Requested**, **Work in Progress**, **Under Review**, **With Client**, **Paid & Completed**, **Internal Overdue** and **External Overdue**. Click one to open those tasks.
- **Outstanding work by team & stage**: a grid of teams against the five workflow stages (Info Requested, Work in Progress, Under Review, With Client, Invoice Pending), with a bottleneck marker and a total per team. Work shared across teams is counted once in its own row. The square colours show volume (1–4, 5–19, 20–99, 100+). Click a square to open those tasks.
- **Internal Adherence** and **External Adherence** (share finished on time), **Invoice Pending** and **At Risk** (due today or within 7 days).

- **History** shows past periods as snapshots: how much work was open at the start and end of the period, and how much was created and completed. There's also a chart of current work by team.
- The team filter narrows every card and the grid to one team.

## Capacity Planning tab
Weekly view: Capacity Load, Hours Allocated, Available Hours and Utilisation, plus a **Workload & status planning grid** per employee (department, team, tasks, progress, hours available and allocated, free time, utilisation) with **Reassign**, filters and **Export CSV**.

## Employee & Services tab
Total capacity, time logged, utilisation, tasks completed, adherence, overdue and at-risk counts, average days to completion, an **Employee Task Breakdown** table (with Export CSV), a **Service Deadline Summary** and **Service Time Analysis by Employee**.

## Timesheet tab
Hours for the chosen period: **Required Hours**, **Logged Hours** (and the share of what was expected), **Variance Hours**, **Utilisation** and **Billable** hours. The table lists each employee's required, logged, billable and non-billable hours, variance and utilisation, with a total row and **Export CSV**. Filters show employees or teams, overruns, and who has or hasn't logged time today.

## Leads tab
**Total Leads**, **New Leads**, **Conversion Rate** and **Avg. Response Time**, a monthly chart of leads against conversions, **Leads Sources**, and a **Leads Breakdown** by employee or by source (total, new, in progress, converted, not responding, with values) and **Recent Leads**. Click a card to open those leads.

## Proposals tab
**Total**, **Sent** and **Signed Proposals** with their value, **Acceptance Rate**, proposals by status, a monthly **Revenue Trend** from signed proposals, average time to sign and proposal value, pending signatures, an **Employee Proposals Breakdown** and **Recent Proposals**.

## Client Summary tab
**Total Clients**, **Active Clients** (with archived and frozen), **New Clients** and **Total Revenue** (annual recurring value). Charts show clients by status, monthly movement (onboarded, left, net growth, value added and lost) and the growth trend. **Clients by Type** breaks the numbers down by client type, with **Export CSV**.

**Status:** First draft (5 Nov 2026), all seven tabs covered 13 Nov 2026 from a preprod walk-through.
