# WhatsApp – Overview

Remindoo lets your practice message clients and leads on WhatsApp from inside the CRM, using your firm's WhatsApp Business number. Chats are linked to the client (and optionally a task or lead), so the conversation shows up on the client's timeline.

*Status (5 Nov 2026): on preprod, being tested under SCRUM-912. Not yet in production.*

## Where to find it
- **Inbox → WhatsApp** (sidebar "Inbox", then the green **WhatsApp** toggle next to Email).
- **Settings → Global Settings → WhatsApp** for numbers, templates, quick replies and retention (admins).

## The WhatsApp inbox
- **Number filter** ("All numbers") if the firm has more than one number.
- **Campaigns** for bulk template sends.
- **New chat** to start a conversation.
- **Search** by name, number or message text.
- Tabs: **All**, **Unread**, **Starred**, **Archived**, **Unassigned**, each with a count.
- Each chat shows the contact, last message, time, any assignees and labels, and whether the 24-hour window is **Open** (with time left) or **Closed**.

## Inside a chat
- Header: contact name and number, window status, **Labels**, **Assigned** (add staff), a star, and a ⋮ menu (**Archive**, **Mark as unread**, side panels **WhatsApp consent** and **Internal notes**).
- Link bar: **Link task**, the linked client, **Link lead**.
- Messages show who sent them ("Sent by …"), time, and ticks (sent, delivered, read). Reactions from the client appear under the message.
- Composer: attach (📎), **quick replies** (⚡), message box, send. Ctrl+Enter also sends.

## Key rules
- **Consent first.** You can't start a chat until the client's WhatsApp consent is recorded (opt-in, how it was given, date, and the agreed wording).
- **24-hour window.** WhatsApp only allows free-text messages for 24 hours after the client last messaged you. Outside that window you can only send an **approved template**.
- **Templates** must be approved by Meta and switched on for the number in Settings.
