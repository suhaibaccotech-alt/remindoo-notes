# Leads — Overview

**Purpose:** What the **Leads** module is for, who uses it, and what you can do with it.

Leads holds prospective clients before they sign up. Sales and partners use it to track enquiries, send emails and proposals, and convert a lead into a client.

## The Leads page
- **Status tabs** with counts: All, Archived, Converted to Client, Not Responding, In-Progress, New Lead, plus any custom statuses your firm adds in **Settings → Lead Settings**.
- **New Lead**, **Show Kanban View**, column visibility, filters and saved filters.
- Columns include Lead Name, Date of Creation, Assignees, Email, Source (e.g. Manual, Website, Xero, QuickBooks, Sage, FreeAgent), Status and Address.

## Working with a lead
Clicking a lead opens a summary (email, status, postcode, lead handler, country, phone, website, notes) with actions:
**Go to Lead Profile**, **Edit Lead Profile**, **Update Lead Status**, **Send Lead Email**, **Add Timeline Note**, **Dial Call** and **Delete Lead**.

## The lead profile and Conversation
**Go to Lead Profile** opens the full record. Its **Conversation** tab is the lead's history in one feed:
- **Notes**: type a note in the Notes box and save it. It appears in the feed straight away.
- **Email**: write to the lead from the composer here. With a connected mailbox the email is kept in the feed as a thread marked **Two way**.
- Events such as status changes and emails sent show in the same feed.

## Filtering leads
Click a status tab to show one status, or use the **funnel** icon to filter by fields such as **Status**, **Source** and **Assignees** (the assignee picker lists employees by name). Filters can be saved, as on every table.

## Custom lead fields
Extra fields for leads are set up in **Settings → Lead Settings**, where you pick each field's type.

## Emailing a lead
**Send Lead Email** lets you:
- **Send from linked account** (your connected mailbox), or **Create campaign** to queue the email for several leads.
- Use lead tokens such as ::Lead Name::, ::Lead Phone Number:: and ::Company Number::.
- Click **Preview Email** to see the email with the lead's details filled in before you send.
