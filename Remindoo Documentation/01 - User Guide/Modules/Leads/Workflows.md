# Leads — Workflows

**Purpose:** Step-by-step instructions for common things you do in **Leads**.

## Create a lead
1. Leads → **New Lead**. *Create Lead* opens on **Basic Information**.
2. Fill in **Lead Name**, **Mobile no.** and **Lead Email** (all compulsory), plus **Notes** and any compulsory custom fields your firm has added. For a company, **Import from Companies House** can fill in the details.
3. **Enter Additional Details** shows more: Company Number, Lead Type, **Assignment Type**, Turnover, Business Website, Country, City, Address, Postal Code and Tags.
4. With Assignment Type set to **Round-Robin**, Remindoo picks who gets the lead when you finish.
5. **Next** moves on to **Contacts** and then **Information & Attachments**. **Save & Exit** saves what you have and closes.

If your firm has an automation on the *New Lead* status (for example a welcome email), it runs as soon as the lead is saved.

## Work a lead
1. Click the lead → **Go to Lead Profile**.
2. Add the people you deal with under **Contacts** and mark one as the **Primary Contact**.
3. Keep the history in **Conversation**: **Add Notes** for calls and meetings, and email from the composer.
4. Use **Create New Task** for follow-ups and **Update Lead Status** as the lead moves on.

## Send a lead a proposal
From the lead's quick view or profile, click **Create Proposal**. See [Proposals — Workflows](../Proposals/Workflows.md).

## Convert a lead to a client
On the lead profile, click **Convert to Client**. The lead's status becomes *Converted to Client*. *The steps after clicking aren't documented yet.*
