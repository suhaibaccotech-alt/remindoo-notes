# Leads — Workflows

**Purpose:** Step-by-step instructions for common things you do in **Leads**.

## Create a lead
*Create Lead* has three steps: **Basic Information**, **Contacts** and **Information & Attachments**.

**Step 1: Basic Information**
1. Leads → **New Lead**.
2. Enter the **Lead Name**.
3. Type the **Mobile no.** and click **+** next to it. The number isn't saved until you click +; it then shows as *Mobile no. #1*. Do the same for the **Lead Email**. You can add more than one of each.
4. Add **Notes** and fill in any custom fields your firm has added. Fields marked * are compulsory.
5. Optionally click **Enter Additional Details** for Company Number, Lead Type, **Assignment Type**, Turnover, Business Website, Country, City, Address, Postal Code and Tags. With Assignment Type set to **Round-Robin**, Remindoo picks who gets the lead. For a company, **Import from Companies House** can fill in the details.
6. **Next**. The lead is saved at this point. **Save & Exit** saves and closes instead.

**Step 2: Contacts**
1. **Create Contact** adds a person at the lead: **Contact Name**, **Contact Email** and **Contact Mobile No.** (all compulsory), with **Enter Additional Details** for the rest.
2. Mark one contact as the **Primary Contact**, or click **Skip** to add contacts later.

**Step 3: Information & Attachments**
1. Enter an **Estimated Value** for the lead if you have one.
2. **Attach File** uploads supporting documents (up to 10 MB each).
3. **Finish**. You'll see "Lead created successfully" and the lead appears at the top of the table with the status *New Lead* and its assignee.

If your firm has an automation on the *New Lead* status (for example a welcome email), it runs when the lead is saved.

## Change a lead's status
Click the lead → **Update Lead Status**. The window shows the **Existing Status**; choose the **New Status** and click **Update**.

## Work a lead
1. Click the lead → **Go to Lead Profile**.
2. Add the people you deal with under **Contacts** and mark one as the **Primary Contact**.
3. Keep the history in **Conversation**: **Add Notes** for calls and meetings, and email from the composer.
4. Use **Create New Task** for follow-ups.

## Send a lead a proposal
From the lead's quick view or profile, click **Create Proposal**. See [Proposals — Workflows](../Proposals/Workflows.md).

## Convert a lead to a client
1. Open the lead profile and click **Convert to Client**.
2. Confirm: *Convert "<lead>" to a client? This creates a new client record and cannot be undone.* Click **Convert**.
3. Wait for "Lead converted to client successfully". It can take around half a minute; don't click again.
4. The lead stays in Leads with the status **Converted to Client**, and can no longer have new proposals created from its Quick Access.
5. A new client with the same name appears in **Clients → Draft**, with its own document folder. Open it and click **Complete your onboarding** to finish setting it up. See [Clients — Workflows](../Clients/Workflows.md).
