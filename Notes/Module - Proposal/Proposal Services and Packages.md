These are called Products and Categories in PandaDoc, within the Catalogue tab.

Services (Products) must have the following:
	Name
	Package (Category)
	Description (Tiny Editor)
	Price
	Cost
	Currency
	Pricing Model
		-Volume-based means that at a certain amount or quantity of product, there will be a different price. This facilitates the bulk pricing discount (Percentage Discount will allow user to specify a percentage discount on a certain quantity of product) or a stepped cost increase impact with the quantity is raised above a certain threshold.
		-Fixed is the Default, one price for however much quantity is ordered.
	Discount Type
		-Flat Amount 
		-Percentage Discount will allow user to specify a percentage discount on a certain quantity of product. It will also require the user to specify the full price of the product.
	Billing Period/Frequency
		One-time means that the entire amount will be billed to the client at once
		Recurring requires the user to specify what frequency will the invoices be sent to the client (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually)
	Custom Fields
		These will be shown in the quote builder or service table as a column in the table for this service. 
		Name
		Value
	Images
	Linkages to Packages
	Custom Product
