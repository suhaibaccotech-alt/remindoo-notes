Unlike the MTD services (where JSON-based submissions are sent to the endpoint documented in the HMRC Developer Hub) the other services that you have listed involve the submission of XML through the Transaction Engine. and also associated or supporting information, such as

Basic guide for XML software developers
Transaction Engine
