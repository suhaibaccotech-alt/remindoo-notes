Thank you for your recent production application request. There is an approvals process for the VAT MTD API, therefore, please follow the instructions below: 

**Understanding your product** 

Please complete questionnaire 1 to enable us to understand your business and the type of product you are offering.  

**Fraud Prevention Headers** 

- You must supply Fraud Prevention Header information for all our APIs, before approval can be granted and production credentials issued. 

- HMRC must see evidence of fraud prevention headers being sent and be satisfied as to their level of accuracy. 

- Please use the [Test Fraud Prevention Headers API](https://developer.service.hmrc.gov.uk/api-documentation/docs/api/service/txm-fph-validator-api/1.0) to check headers submitted by your application meet the latest version of the [Fraud Prevention Headers specification](https://developer.service.hmrc.gov.uk/guides/fraud-prevention). 

- You can find information about the status of your Fraud Prevention Headers by [logging into your Developer Hub account](https://developer.service.hmrc.gov.uk/developer/login) and choosing ‘Fraud Prevention’. This includes details of errors and any potential improvements. 

Our technical partners will then evaluate your Fraud Prevention Header (FPH) testing. Please be aware that we will be unable to progress your Production application until the minimum standards are met. 

**Accessibility**  

**Questionnaire 2 (WCAG 2.1 AA)**  

**Note: Please only complete this questionnaire if your product is for retail/commercial use and you intend to sell/licence the product.1** 

Our [Terms of Use](https://developer.service.hmrc.gov.uk/api-documentation/docs/terms-of-use) specify that web-based software must meet level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG). Desktop software should follow the equivalent offline standards. 

To help us understand if your software meets WCAG guidelines, please complete the Questionnaire 2 (WCAG 2.1 AA) checklist. Please answer all questions. If you answer ‘Partially Supports’ or ‘Not Applicable’ to any question, please provide a narrative in the ‘Remarks and Explanations’ column to help us understand why your product Partially Supports a criterion, or why that criterion is not relevant to your software. 

Once we receive your completed checklist and have reviewed your submitted answers, we can provide feedback on the accessibility of your software product. 

**Testing requirements**

Once your application has passed our full FPH Evaluation we will analyse the testing done using your Sandbox application details, based upon the answers you have provided in Questionnaire 1.
